Tracked at every step of a real fast-path settlement (raw data in
studionet-fund-trace-2026-10-07.json):

  start            client=0  worker=0  contract=0
  after create     client=0  worker=0  contract=1000
  after submit     client=0  worker=0  contract=1000
  immediately      client=0  worker=0  contract=1000
  +10s             client=0  worker=0  contract=1000
  +20s             client=0  worker=0  contract=0
  +40s             client=0  worker=0  contract=0

The escrow leaves the contract (correct, and this is what the test asserts), but the worker
EOA is never credited.

Root cause, confirmed rather than inferred: `accept_deliverable` emits an external message
to the worker's chain-layer address. The resulting child transaction is
  0xa919c47c6872ed1c778cc1752e1c3cd2078d348bb0702cb0a05c6efc50fdde1d
with to=0xF309CEfDcC8F24e8d880a43e7cf34Ae48add50F4, value=1000, status=FINALIZED (7),
leader execution_result=ERROR, votes={} — no validator voted. Per the docs, "If the child
transaction fails, the value is not automatically returned to the sender", and "Studio:
Balances are simulated in a local database. There is no EVM layer or ghost contracts in
Studio." An Intelligent Contract transfer to a chain-layer address therefore has nowhere to
land on Studionet.

Related observation: the client account also shows a 0 balance after paying 1000 in, so
account-level GEN conservation cannot be demonstrated on this network at all. The contract's
own `_assert_escrow_conserved` invariant is intact and the deployed contract settles to 0x0,
so this is a network/EOA-layer limitation, not Docket escrow logic.

Consequence for the test suite: the worker-credit assertion is gated behind
`_eoa_credit_supported`, with the notice printed on the run so it is never mistaken for a
pass. Contract-side escrow conservation is still asserted unconditionally.

CONSEQUENCE FOR CLAIMS: on Studionet, Docket demonstrates that adjudication and escrow
*accounting* are correct and consensus-final. It does NOT demonstrate that GEN reaches a
recipient's wallet. Any submission or demo must say so explicitly. Re-verify on Bradbury
before claiming real payouts, and remove the network from NETWORKS_WITHOUT_EOA_CREDIT only
after observing a credited balance there.
