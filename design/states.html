This dispute is undetermined

Validators didn't reach majority agreement after all leader rotations. This docket will re-enter adjudication with a new leader automatically — no action needed from you.

If this keeps happening, it usually means the acceptance criteria are too ambiguous for validators to evaluate consistently. Consider that for future dockets.
