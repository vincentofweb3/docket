Connect a wallet to continue

Docket reads the register without a wallet. You'll need one to post, claim, submit, accept, or dispute a docket.

Network: this instance points at bradbury (chain id 4221). If your wallet is on another network, Docket will prompt you to switch before your first write action — reads work on any network.
new to GenLayer?

You'll need testnet GEN to post or claim work on Bradbury. Get some from the GenLayer faucet — it's a manual, browser-based claim, so do this before you try your first transaction.
